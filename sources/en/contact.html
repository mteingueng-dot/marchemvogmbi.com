FAQ

Good to know

Another question? Call us at 819 239-9864 or email contact@marchemvogmbi.com.

Where can I find prices?

Prices are displayed in store. To know the price of a product, call us at 819 239-9864 and we will tell you right away. And with our best price guarantee, you always pay less than elsewhere.

How do I know if a product is available?

Arrivals change often, especially for fresh products. Call us before coming in for a specific product.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Call us at 819 239-9864 to place your order. Delivery is free for any order of $100 or more within 5 km of the store.

How does the best price guarantee work?

If you find the same product, in the same size, cheaper at another store, we will sell it to you for 1¢ less. And if you already bought it from us, we will credit you the difference on your previous bill. Bring the other store’s receipt or flyer.

Can I pay online?

Not yet: the online shop is for discovering our products. Payment is made in store or on delivery.

What does “by weight” mean?

Some fresh products are sold by weight: they are weighed in store to the quantity you want.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
