Puerto Rico

Mofongo

The Puerto Rican dish: fried green plantains pounded with garlic and crispy pork, served with broth.

  • Time : 45 min
  • Serves : 4 people

Ingredients

  • 4 green plantainsFind in the shop →
  • 6 garlic clovesFind in the shop →
  • 150 g fried pork rinds or bacon (chicharrón)Find in the shop →
  • 3 tablespoons olive oilFind in the shop →
  • 250 ml chicken broth, salt; oil for fryingFind in the shop →

Method

  1. Peel the plantains, cut them into 2 cm chunks and fry for 8 minutes over medium heat without browning too much.
  2. Pound the garlic with salt and olive oil in a mortar.
  3. Add the hot plantains a little at a time and pound with the crispy pork.
  4. Drizzle with a little broth so the mofongo stays moist.
  5. Shape into domes and serve with the rest of the hot broth.
