Tunisia

Shakshuka

Eggs poached in a tomato and bell pepper sauce with cumin and harissa, to mop up with bread.

  • Time : 40 min
  • Serves : 4 people

Ingredients

  • 6 tomatoes (or 1 can crushed tomatoes)Find in the shop →
  • 3 bell peppers, 1 onion, 3 garlic clovesFind in the shop →
  • 6 eggsFind in the shop →
  • 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 of paprikaFind in the shop →
  • 1 teaspoon harissa, olive oil, salt; bread to serveFind in the shop →

Method

  1. Fry the onion and sliced peppers in olive oil for 10 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika and harissa; cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes and salt; simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Make wells in the sauce and crack the eggs into them.
  5. Cover and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, until the whites are set. Serve with bread.
