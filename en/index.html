Your African, Caribbean and Latin grocery in Sherbrooke

Looking for gari, attiéké, ndolé leaves, red palm oil or smoked fish in Sherbrooke? Marché Mvog-Mbi, at 3071 boulevard Portland, carries over 2,000 products from Central and West Africa, the Caribbean and Latin America: plantains, yam, cocoyam and taro, fufu and cassava flours, spices such as Penja pepper, meat, drinks, beauty care and braiding hair.

Whether you come from Cameroon, Côte d’Ivoire, Congo, Senegal, Haiti, Jamaica, Colombia or Quebec, you will find the ingredients of your favourite dishes here, along with step-by-step recipes to discover new ones. We are open every day from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Delivery is free on orders from $100 within 5 km, and our best price guarantee means you pay 1¢ less than elsewhere for the same product.

Looking for something specific? Call us at 819 239-9864 before you come: new arrivals change often.
