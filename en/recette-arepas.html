Colombia and Venezuela

Cheese arepas

The golden corn cakes of Colombia and Venezuela, crisp outside, soft inside, filled with cheese.

  • Time : 30 min
  • Serves : 8 arepas

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pre-cooked corn flour (masarepa)Find in the shop →
  • 2½ cups warm water, 1 tsp saltFind in the shop →
  • 200 g white cheese (queso blanco or costeño)Find in the shop →
  • Butter or oilFind in the shop →
  • Or ready-made arepas to reheatFind in the shop →

Method

  1. Mix the warm water and salt, add the flour little by little and knead into a smooth dough; rest 5 minutes.
  2. Add half the grated cheese to the dough (optional).
  3. Shape 8 balls and flatten into 1.5 cm thick cakes.
  4. Cook in a buttered pan over medium heat 6 to 8 minutes per side, until they sound hollow.
  5. Split on the side, fill with cheese and serve hot.
