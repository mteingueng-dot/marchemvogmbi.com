Cameroon

Sweet okok

The Beti dish of central and southern Cameroon: finely cut okok leaves cooked in palm nut juice and peanut paste, served with cassava sticks.

  • Time : 1 h
  • Serves : 6 people

Ingredients

  • 500 g finely cut okok (eru) leavesFind in the shop →
  • 300 g peanut pasteFind in the shop →
  • 1 litre palm nut juice (or 800 g palm nut cream)Find in the shop →
  • 2 tablespoons sugarFind in the shop →
  • Cassava sticks (bobolo) to serveFind in the shop →

Method

  1. Wash the okok leaves and soak them for 10 minutes in warm water; drain.
  2. Bring the palm nut juice to a boil in a large pot.
  3. Thin the peanut paste with a little hot juice, add it and cook for 15 minutes, stirring.
  4. Add the okok, sugar and a pinch of salt; cook for 30 minutes over low heat, stirring, until the sauce thickens and the oil rises.
  5. Serve hot with cassava sticks.
