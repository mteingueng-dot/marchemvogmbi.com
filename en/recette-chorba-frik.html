Algeria

Chorba frik

Algeria’s Ramadan soup: lamb, tomato and cracked green wheat, flavoured with cilantro and mint.

  • Time : 1 h 15
  • Serves : 6 people

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb in small piecesFind in the shop →
  • 100 g frik (cracked green wheat)Find in the shop →
  • 1 can chickpeasFind in the shop →
  • 2 grated tomatoes and 1 tablespoon tomato pasteFind in the shop →
  • 1 onion, 1 bunch cilantro, dried mint, cinnamon, paprika, salt, pepperFind in the shop →

Method

  1. Brown the lamb and chopped onion in a little oil with the cinnamon, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Add the tomatoes, tomato paste, half the chopped cilantro and 2 litres of water; cook for 40 minutes.
  3. Add the drained chickpeas and sprinkle in the frik; cook for 20 minutes, stirring now and then.
  4. Add the rest of the cilantro and a pinch of dried mint.
  5. Serve with a lemon wedge, dates and bread.
