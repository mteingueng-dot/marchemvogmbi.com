Cameroon

Mbongo tchobi (black spice stew)

The black sauce of the Bassa people: fish cooked in a sauce of roasted mbongo spices, deep and fragrant, served with plantain or miondo.

  • Time : 1 h 15
  • Serves : 6 people

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg fish (sea bass, carp or tilapia) in steaksFind in the shop →
  • 100 g mbongo spicesFind in the shop →
  • 2 tomatoes, 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, gingerFind in the shop →
  • 100 ml palm oilFind in the shop →
  • Chili, 1 stock cube, saltFind in the shop →
  • Boiled green plantains or miondoFind in the shop →

Method

  1. Dry-roast the mbongo in a pan until well blackened (without burning), then grind it.
  2. Blend the tomato, onion, garlic, ginger and ground mbongo with a little water.
  3. Heat the palm oil, pour in the purée and cook 10 minutes, stirring.
  4. Add 500 ml water, stock cube, salt and chili; bring to the boil.
  5. Lay in the cleaned fish steaks and cook 20 minutes over low heat, without stirring, shaking the pot.
  6. Serve with boiled plantains or miondo.
