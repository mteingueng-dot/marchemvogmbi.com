Ecuador

Bolón de verde

Green plantain balls from Ecuador’s coast, mashed with cheese and crispy pork, then browned in a pan.

  • Time : 40 min
  • Serves : 4 people

Ingredients

  • 4 green plantainsFind in the shop →
  • 150 g grilling cheese or mozzarella, dicedFind in the shop →
  • 150 g chicharrón (fried pork) or baconFind in the shop →
  • 2 tablespoons butter or lardFind in the shop →
  • ½ teaspoon achiote (annatto), saltFind in the shop →

Method

  1. Cut the plantains into pieces and boil them in salted water for 20 minutes.
  2. Brown the pork in small pieces and keep the fat.
  3. Mash the hot plantains with the butter, achiote and a little of the fat.
  4. Add the cheese and pork, then shape into orange-sized balls.
  5. Brown them in a pan with a little oil. Serve for breakfast with an egg and coffee.
