Cape Verde

Cachupa

Cape Verde’s national dish: corn and beans slowly simmered with meats, chorizo, cassava, sweet potato and cabbage.

  • Time : 2 h 30
  • Serves : 8 people

Ingredients

  • 500 g cracked hominy corn (samp)Find in the shop →
  • 300 g red beans or broad beansFind in the shop →
  • 800 g pork and chicken, 1 chorizoFind in the shop →
  • 300 g cassava and 1 sweet potatoFind in the shop →
  • ½ cabbage, 2 onions, 4 garlic cloves, bay leaf, saltFind in the shop →

Method

  1. Soak the corn and beans overnight.
  2. Boil them for 1 hour with the bay leaf.
  3. Fry the onions, garlic and meats in a little oil, then add them to the pot with the chorizo.
  4. Add the cassava, sweet potato and cabbage in chunks; cook for another hour over low heat.
  5. Salt and serve hot; the next day, cachupa is eaten fried again with an egg.
