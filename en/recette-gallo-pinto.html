Costa Rica and Nicaragua

Gallo pinto

Central America’s breakfast: day-old rice stir-fried with black beans, onion, bell pepper and cilantro.

  • Time : 30 min
  • Serves : 4 people

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked black beans, with a little of their liquidFind in the shop →
  • 3 cups white rice cooked the day beforeFind in the shop →
  • 1 onion and 1 red bell pepper, chopped, 2 garlic clovesFind in the shop →
  • 1 bunch cilantroFind in the shop →
  • 2 tablespoons oil, salt; Lizano or Worcestershire sauce to tasteFind in the shop →

Method

  1. Fry the onion, bell pepper and garlic in the oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the beans and their liquid; let reduce for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the rice and stir gently until it takes on the colour of the beans.
  4. Season with salt and a dash of Lizano sauce.
  5. Sprinkle with cilantro and serve with eggs, fried ripe plantain and sour cream.
