Our story

A Yaoundé market in the heart of Sherbrooke

Our name comes from Mvog-Mbi, a neighbourhood of Yaoundé known for its big market, where people come from all over the city and far beyond to shop, chat and trade.

That is the spirit we wanted to bring to 3071 boulevard Portland: a place where everyone finds the products of their home country and discovers those of others. Along the aisles, families from Central and West Africa meet families from the Caribbean, Latin America and Québec.

Today, over 2,000 products fill our shelves, from fresh plantains to smoked fish, from flours to beauty care.

A crossroads of cultures

Four great cuisines share our aisles. People come for the products of their childhood and leave with a recipe learned from a neighbour of another culture.
