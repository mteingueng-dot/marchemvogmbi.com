Brazil

Feijoada

Brazil’s great stew of black beans simmered with several cuts of pork and sausage, served with rice and oranges.

  • Time : 3 h (+ overnight soaking)
  • Serves : 8 people

Ingredients

  • 1 kg black (turtle) beansFind in the shop →
  • 1.5 kg pork: ribs, shoulder, trotter or tailFind in the shop →
  • 400 g chorizo or smoked sausageFind in the shop →
  • 2 onions, 1 head of garlic, bay leavesFind in the shop →
  • White rice, oranges, sautéed collard greensFind in the shop →
  • Toasted cassava flour (farofa)Find in the shop →

Method

  1. Soak the beans overnight; desalt any salted meats.
  2. Cook the beans with the bay leaves in plenty of water for 1 hour.
  3. Brown the meats in pieces and the chorizo, then add them to the beans.
  4. Fry the onions and garlic, add a ladle of beans, mash and return to the pot.
  5. Simmer 1 h 30 over low heat until the sauce is thick and the meats are tender.
  6. Serve with rice, sautéed greens, farofa and orange wedges.
