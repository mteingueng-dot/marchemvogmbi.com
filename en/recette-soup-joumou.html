Haiti

Soup joumou

The freedom soup served in Haiti every January 1st: a pumpkin soup with beef, vegetables and pasta.

  • Time : 2 h 30
  • Serves : 8 people

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef soup bones and meatFind in the shop →
  • 1 pumpkin or squash of about 1.5 kgFind in the shop →
  • Cabbage, carrots, potatoes, turnip, leekFind in the shop →
  • Epis, thyme, cloves, 1 chiliFind in the shop →
  • 200 g pasta (macaroni or spaghetti)Find in the shop →
  • Lime juice to wash the meatFind in the shop →

Method

  1. Wash the meat with lime juice, marinate in epis, then cook in 3 litres of water for 1 hour.
  2. Cook the peeled pumpkin separately, then blend with a little broth and add to the pot.
  3. Add the cut vegetables, thyme, cloves and whole chili; cook 30 minutes.
  4. Add the pasta and cook 10 more minutes.
  5. Adjust the salt and serve hot with a slice of bread.
